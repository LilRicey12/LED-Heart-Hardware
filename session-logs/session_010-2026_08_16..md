## Problems Encountered

* NE555 was not outputting a voltage
* Circuit was behaving weirdly when I attached the CD4017
* I wasn't getting a voltage to the LED despite the output having a correct voltage
* My LEDs where not matching with the numbers of outputs I had


## Troubleshooting Process

* I'm actually not to sure why this happened. It was fixed by disconnecting the CONTROL pin from ground, I had it initial through a small capacitor then to ground. By simply removing it it made the circuit work perfectly
* Stupid little issue, I had the CD4017 placed backwards, my guess is that the way the pins where lined up it was causing a really high load which was causing issues
* Another stupid little issue, I didn't have the CD4017 placed deep enough into the breadboard. This caused a proper reading on the pin, but the current didn't go to the LED
* This one is as simple as engineering students can't count. I accidently used 18 LEDs instead of the full 20. I just ended up connected Q9 to the RESET pin, this was because I didn't have space on the breadboard to neetly add 2 more LEDs
