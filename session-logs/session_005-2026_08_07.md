## Troubleshooting Process 2

* I had to do some research into capacitors, this taught me that the current doesn't flow through the capacitor like a diode or resistor. Rather the capacitor consists of 2 plates seperator by a plate where ideally the charge can't cross. One side has the stored voltage and in really simple terms the other plate attracts the voltage to go into the other plate. All in all when discharge pin is active both plates are in a GND state so the current flows into it
* This was very related to learning how capacitors work, using test data, and studying the wiring schematic. Once all of that was done it wasn't to hard to figure out what each resistor does
