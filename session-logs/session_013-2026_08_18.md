## Troubleshooting Process

* Who ever made solder wick needs a nobel prize
* I had to use a small solid core wire to bridge across the lifted pad
* This was actually a large headache. By the time I noticed all the LEDs and capacitors were wired to "GND". It became a decision of undo all of that work, or improivse my design. I ended up improvising my design, the to IC circuits are now slightly different then how they are in the layout. Depending on how things go I plan to update the layout with the new design before closing the project
* This was annoying as well. I was checking the output with a multimetre and the value kept oscillating between 3.1 and 3.7. I found this odd so I made a small led circuit on a breadboard and connected it to the output and it worked perfectly. After some research I found that my multimetre could not keep up with how fast the oscillating was so it was averaging a value. That is what caused the weird readings.
